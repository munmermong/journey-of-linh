A journey of me

01. About Me

My name is Phuong Linh. People may assume Phuong is my first name, but in Vietnamese, the name order is reversed — Linh is actually my first name! I'm energetic, love bringing joy to others and am definitely a food lover (someone may find me annoying as I keep asking for a bite of their food). I enjoy discovering interesting things around me like watching my little terrarium change every day or listening to NewJeans.

02. About My Journey

I'm a student at both the University of Technology Sydney (UTS) and Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HCMUT) through the Transnational Education (TNE) programme, which connects the two universities. This gives me the chance to experience an Australian-style education while continuing to live in Vietnam, close to my family and friends.
