My Future

A future full of possibilities.

01. My Vision

I'm still exploring which direction I want to take in computing. Artificial intelligence interests me, especially how it can be applied to different fields. Rather than choosing one path too early, I want to experience different areas and discover what suits me best. My ambitions have also grown beyond simply finding a good job and earning a comfortable living. I hope to create opportunities for myself, gain meaningful experiences and explore what the world has to offer.

02. Career Aspirations

I have a few directions I'd love to explore as I move forward. They are not fixed plans, but possibilities that motivate me.

Stage My Aspirations
During University Find a part-time job to gain working experience and become more financially independent.
After Graduation Work for an international company, learn from different people and experience a professional working environment.
Long-term Dreams Study and live abroad, and perhaps build a business of my own one day.

03. My Future Goals

During university, I want to make the most of the opportunities around me, both inside and outside the classroom. Besides achieving good academic results, I hope to strengthen my technical skills, improve my communication and leadership abilities, and gain practical experience. I also want to meet people with similar interests, join competitions and work on personal projects. Most importantly, I want to keep challenging myself and make these years meaningful.

  • Strengthen my technical skills and knowledge.
  • Improve my communication and leadership abilities.
  • Gain experience through competitions and personal projects.
  • Build connections and learn from others.

04. The Road Ahead

I know my future may not turn out exactly as I imagine, and I'm open to new possibilities. I don't need every step planned out yet. I simply want to stay curious, have the courage to try unfamiliar things and learn from my experiences. Whatever direction I take, I hope to make the most of every opportunity and keep growing, one step at a time.
