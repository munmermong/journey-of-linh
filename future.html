My Future

A vision of what lies ahead

Dreams • Goals • Aspirations

01. My Vision

I'm still exploring which direction I want to take in computing. Artificial intelligence interests me, especially how it can be applied to different fields. Rather than choosing one path too early, I want to explore different areas and discover what suits me best.

My ambitions have also grown beyond simply finding a good job and earning a comfortable living. I hope to create opportunities for myself, gain meaningful experiences and explore what the world has to offer.

02. Career Aspirations

I have a few directions I'd love to explore as I move forward. They are not fixed plans, but possibilities that motivate me.

Stage My Aspirations
During University Find a part-time job to gain working experience and become more financially independent.
After Graduation Work for an international company, learn from different people and experience a professional working environment.
Long-term Dreams Study and live abroad, and perhaps build a business of my own one day.

03. My Future Goals

During university, I want to make the most of the opportunities around me, both inside and outside the classroom. Beyond academic results, I hope to grow in different areas:

Technical Skills

Strengthen my knowledge and become more confident in applying what I learn.

Personal Development

Improve my communication, leadership and teamwork abilities.

Practical Experience

Take part in competitions and personal projects to put my knowledge into practice.

Connections

Meet people with similar interests and learn from their experiences.

04. The Road Ahead

I know my future may not turn out exactly as I imagine, and I'm open to new possibilities. I don't need every step planned out right now. I simply want to stay curious, have the courage to try unfamiliar things and learn from my experiences.

Whatever direction I take, I hope to make the most of every opportunity and keep growing, one step at a time.
