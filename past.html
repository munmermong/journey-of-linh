My Past

Every journey has a beginning.

01. Where It Began

My relationship with computers started quite casually. At first, they were simply a wonderful place where I could play video games, watch YouTube and explore the online world. Over time, I realised that I could learn how to use new software, websites and applications very quickly, without feeling overwhelmed. I was always curious about what I could do with technology and enjoyed finding creative ways to use it. Whether it was making presentations, working with Word or creating something online, I often found myself performing well without much difficulty. Interestingly, computing was never really my childhood dream. I once imagined myself becoming an English teacher, and I enjoyed almost every school subject equally. I simply loved computers, even before I knew they would become such an important part of my journey.

02. My Learning Journey

My experience with computing gradually grew as I explored different tools and programming languages throughout secondary school and high school. I first encountered coding through Excel, followed by Pascal, Python, SQL, HTML and CSS. Although I had a few setbacks along the way, I found that I could understand new languages quite quickly and learn them without feeling overwhelmed. Outside programming, I also became comfortable using tools such as Word, PowerPoint, Excel and Canva, especially when creating presentations and other digital work. Now, as a TNE student, I'm getting more opportunities to explore computing beyond the classroom. I'm currently learning Python and Java while working on projects such as an app that detects scam calls and a system designed to simplify bus fare payments. I've also started getting involved in technology-related competitions, including the OISP Presentation Contest. Even though my role isn't directly focused on the technical side, I get to learn from my friends and gain experience that I can apply to future competitions.

Stage What I Explored What I Gained
Secondary School Excel and Pascal My first experience with coding and logical thinking.
High School Python, SQL, HTML and CSS Experience with programming languages and web development.
University Python, Java and technology projects Applying knowledge through projects and competitions.

03. Skills & Discoveries

Through my experiences with technology, I've discovered that one of my greatest strengths is my curiosity. I enjoy exploring new websites, applications and software, often learning how they work simply by trying things out. I'm also adaptable and can usually pick up new tools and programming languages quite quickly. When facing a problem, I can try to figure things out independently, but I don't always stick to one approach. Sometimes, asking for help, learning from others or experimenting with different solutions works better. I believe this flexibility, together with my creativity and willingness to explore, helps me feel comfortable in the constantly changing world of technology.

Some Skills I've Developed

  • Learning new software and digital tools independently.
  • Adapting to different programming languages.
  • Creating presentations and visual materials.
  • Exploring different solutions when facing a problem.

04. What I Have Learned

Looking back, computing has taught me much more than just technical skills. It has helped me become more confident, independent, patient and open to new experiences. I used to imagine myself becoming an English teacher, but as time went by, my curiosity about computers gradually grew into a genuine interest in science and technology. The more I explored, the more I wanted to learn. This change also influenced the way I approach studying. I've learned not to let the fear of something being difficult stop me from trying. If I had always believed that I couldn't understand computing, I might never have discovered how much I enjoy it today. Now, I see challenges as opportunities to learn, improve and discover what I'm capable of.
