01. About Me

Hi! I'm Phuong Linh, although you might assume Phuong is my first name because it comes first. In Vietnamese, however, names are written in a different order, so Linh is actually my given name! I'm an energetic person who loves bringing joy and laughter to the people around me. I'm also a big food lover — so don't be surprised if I keep asking for a bite of your food! I find almost everything around me interesting and believe there's always something new to discover. Whether it's watching my little terrarium change day by day, exploring something unfamiliar, or listening to NewJeans, I enjoy finding happiness in the little things.

02. About My Journey

My journey in computing is shaped by a unique learning experience. I'm currently studying at both the University of Technology Sydney (UTS) and Ho Chi Minh City University of Technology (HCMUT) through a Transnational Education (TNE) programme. This partnership gives me the opportunity to experience an Australian-style education, develop new skills and broaden my perspective, while still living in Vietnam and staying close to my family and friends. It's a journey that allows me to explore new opportunities, challenge myself and gradually discover what I want to pursue in the world of computing.
