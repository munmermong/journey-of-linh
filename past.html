01. Where It Began

My interest in computers started with simple things like playing games, watching YouTube and exploring the online world. I soon discovered that I could learn new software and applications quickly, and I enjoyed experimenting with what technology could do. Although I once dreamed of becoming an English teacher, computers had already become a big part of my everyday life.

02. My Learning Journey

From my first coding experiences to university projects, each stage introduced me to new tools and opportunities.

Stage Tools & Languages Experience
Secondary School Excel, Pascal My first steps into coding and logical thinking.
High School Python, SQL, HTML, CSS Exploring programming and web development.
University Python, Java Developing projects and joining technology competitions.

Beyond the Classroom

I'm currently exploring projects such as a scam-call detection app and a bus payment system. I have also joined the OISP Presentation Contest, learning from my teammates along the way.

03. Skills & Discoveries

Curiosity

Enjoying exploration and discovering how things work.

Adaptability

Picking up new tools and technologies quickly.

Creativity

Finding different ways to use technology.

Flexibility

Knowing when to work independently and when to learn from others.

04. What I Have Learned

Computing has helped me become more confident, independent and open to new experiences. What started as casual curiosity has gradually grown into a genuine interest in science and technology.

"Don't let the fear of something being difficult stop you from trying." — Just made this up

I've learned that challenges are part of the process, and that every new experience is an opportunity to discover what I can do.
